# Trademark policy

**panoma — trademark and brand usage policy, v1.0**

The code in this repository is free software under the AGPL-3.0-only. **The name and the
logo are not part of that grant.**

That is not a loophole; it is how every open-source licence works. Copyright licences
give you the right to copy, change and redistribute the *code*. Trademarks are a separate
body of law, and open-source licences overwhelmingly exclude them rather than include
them — the AGPL-3.0 says so itself in section 7(e), which lets a licensor decline to
grant rights to use its names, trademarks or logos.

The reason to write this down is not to take anything back. It is so that a person
downloading something called "panoma" knows where it came from. Software that reads your
entire disk and promises that nothing leaves it is exactly the kind of software where the
name has to mean something.

## What you may always do, with no permission

- **Use, modify, fork and redistribute the code**, under the AGPL. That is the licence,
  and nothing here narrows it.
- **Say what your thing is.** "Based on panoma", "a fork of panoma", "compatible with
  panoma", "panoma plugin", "works with panoma". Referring to a product by its name in
  order to describe it truthfully is nominative use, and it is fine.
- **Redistribute panoma unmodified** — the npm package, a distro package, a Docker
  image — under the name panoma, as long as it really is panoma and you make clear you
  are not the publisher.
- **Write about it.** Articles, talks, courses, screenshots, reviews, videos, comparisons.
  Including harsh ones.
- **Use the logo to point at us** — to link to this project, to illustrate a piece about
  it, or to show that your product integrates with it.

## What needs permission

- **Naming your own product, fork, distribution, hosted service or company "panoma"**, or
  a name close enough to be confused with it.
- **Registering a domain, social account, package name, app-store listing or organisation
  name** built around "panoma".
- **Using the logo as your own** — as your icon, in your branding, or in a way that
  suggests we published, endorsed or reviewed your work.
- **Modified builds distributed under the name.** Fork all you like; ship it under your
  own name. This is the ordinary rule for copyleft projects: Firefox/IceWeasel exists for
  exactly this reason.

Ask at **support@panoma.ai**. Permission is usually a short conversation, and it is
often yes.

## The plain-language version

> You may always say your software is **based on panoma**.
> You may not call it **panoma**.

## Scope and status

This policy covers the word mark **panoma** and the P monogram in
`apps/web/public/assets/brand/`. Trademark rights in the United States arise from use as
well as registration; registration status may change, and this document does not claim
any registration that has not been granted.

This is a usage policy, not legal advice, and it does not modify the AGPL-3.0. Where the
two ever appear to conflict about the *code*, the licence governs — this document only
ever speaks about the *name*.
